REALAi Plate™ for Governments & Manufacturers

Modernizing vehicle identity for the 21st century

A sovereign, privacy-first digital license plate system designed for states, DMVs, and automotive manufacturers.

For State Governments & DMVs

Key Benefits

  • • Drastically reduce license plate fraud, cloning, and theft
  • • Enable real-time, forensically verifiable enforcement
  • • Lower administrative costs and manual processing
  • • Improve public safety through immutable location records
  • • Seamless integration with existing tolling and traffic systems

Technical Advantages

REALAi Plate™ replaces 100-year-old static metal plates with a dynamic, Bio-Sign™-secured digital identity layer that is:

  • • Daily rotating low-power E-Ink public code
  • • Cryptographically bound to vehicle VIN and owner Bio-Sign™
  • • Immutable audit trail in the Bureau ledger
  • • Zero-knowledge proofs for privacy-preserving verification

For Automotive Manufacturers

Embed sovereign vehicle identity at the factory level — future-proofing connected and autonomous vehicles.

  • • Native integration with connected car platforms
  • • $AII Compute Economy participation for vehicles
  • • Simplified compliance with future federal and state regulations
  • • Enhanced owner experience and data sovereignty
