About REALAiiD

We exist to give every person true ownership and protection of their identity in the digital age.

REALAiiD was founded by Nehemiah Brown with one clear purpose: to help people take back control of their identity in a world that is becoming increasingly digital.

As artificial intelligence grows more powerful, the risk of identity theft, deepfakes, and digital impersonation has never been greater. Traditional IDs, passwords, and plastic cards are no longer enough. We believe every person deserves a secure, private, and sovereign digital identity that belongs to them — not to corporations or governments.

Our Mission

We are building the Bureau of Digital Identity — a trusted system that allows individuals to prove who they are without giving up their privacy. Our products turn your physical self into a protected digital presence. We do not sell your data. We do not share your biometric information. Your identity is yours alone, and we exist to protect it.

Why This Matters

Everything in life is moving online — banking, travel, healthcare, work, and social connections. Without strong personal identity protection, individuals become vulnerable. REALAiiD gives you the tools to step confidently into this new digital world with full ownership and control.

Our Promise to You

Your biometric data is never sold or shared with third parties. It is used only to verify that you are you — when and where you choose. We are building this company to serve people, not exploit them.

Our Products

Every REALAiiD product is designed with one goal in mind: to protect and empower your identity.

  • Digital Human Identity — Secure your personal information and likeness.
  • REALAi Plate™ — Modern vehicle identity that protects your privacy on the road.
  • Wallet Integration — Make payments using your biology instead of passwords.
  • Public Safety Credentials — Give trusted professionals verifiable authority.
  • $AII Compute Economy — Earn real value from your verified presence and activity.

REALAiiD is more than technology. It is a movement to bring dignity, security, and ownership back to the individual in the digital age.
