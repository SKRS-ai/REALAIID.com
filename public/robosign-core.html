Department of Identity for Robotics

A "rogue" robot is simply an unidentified one. RoboSign™ provides the foundational infrastructure to convert autonomous machines from liabilities into logged, verifiable, and responsible assets within the REALAiID ecosystem.

For Manufacturers: Registry Integration

Manufacturers leverage the RoboSign™ API to embed a unique 'Forensic DNA' into the firmware of every chassis at the assembly stage.

  • Immutable Hardware Hashing: Ensures the robot's physical sensor suite and computational core are inextricably linked to its digital ID.
  • Lifecycle Auditing: From factory output to final decommissioning, every state change is logged in the Bureau’s permanent ledger.

For Owners: Total Operational Oversight

As an owner, RoboSign™ transforms your autonomous fleet into controlled, sovereign assets.

  • Geofence Enforcement: Verify the operational boundaries of your autonomous assets in real-time.
  • Biometric Master Override: Only verified human-signature intent can authorize core firmware updates or emergency shutdowns.
  • Accountability Tether: Every unit functions as an extension of your own verified identity, ensuring regulatory compliance and industrial safety.
